Data Deletion

You can request deletion of an Afro Intelligent account, workspace, connected-service data or specific personal information.

How to request deletion

  1. Email privacy@afrointelligent.co.za from the address connected to your account.
  2. Use the subject Data deletion request.
  3. State whether you want an account, workspace, conversation, document or connected-service record deleted.
  4. We will verify that you are the account owner or an authorised person before acting.

What happens next

We acknowledge the request, verify authority, disconnect applicable integrations and delete or anonymise eligible information. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days. We may retain limited records where required for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations or disputes.

Meta and connected providers

Deleting Afro Intelligent data does not automatically delete information held independently by Meta, a payment provider or another connected service. We will explain any direct provider step that remains necessary.
