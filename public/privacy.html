Privacy Policy

How Afro Intelligent collects, uses, protects and deletes information when businesses use our automation workspace.

Information we process

  • Account, business-profile and contact information supplied by workspace users.
  • Business verification information and private documents uploaded by authorised owners or administrators.
  • Customer conversation and transaction metadata processed for connected services.
  • Security, audit, device and operational logs needed to protect and operate the service.

How information is used

We use information to provide the requested service, authenticate users, maintain tenant isolation, operate integrations, support customers, prevent abuse and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not send verification documents or payment credentials to AI models.

Sharing and processors

Information is shared only with authorised service providers and integrations needed to deliver the service, or when required by law. When a business connects Meta or another provider, that provider processes information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Security and retention

We use access controls, encrypted transport, tenant-scoped records, private document storage, audit logging and encrypted credential storage. Information is retained only while needed for the service, security, disputes and legal requirements.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal information. Workspace owners remain responsible for the lawful collection and use of their customers' information.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to privacy@afrointelligent.co.za.
